## How to Raise a Grievance

Email the Grievance Officer (or `care@spattoo.com`) with:
1. your name and contact details;
2. a clear description of the issue and the URL or storefront/order it relates to;
3. what outcome you are seeking; and
4. for content-takedown or infringement requests, the details and any proof required
   under applicable law, and a statement that the information provided is accurate.

## Our Response Timelines

- We will **acknowledge** your grievance within **24 hours**.
- We will **resolve** it within the timelines required by law — generally **within 15
  days**, and sooner for categories that require expedited action under the applicable
  rules.

## Escalation

If you are not satisfied with the resolution of a personal-data grievance, you may
escalate to the **Data Protection Board of India** as provided under the DPDP Act.
